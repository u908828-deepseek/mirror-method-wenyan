## How to Fill In a Masterfile 📝

A Masterfile is your personal document that describes you and your method of work. It is needed so that Arch understands who it is talking to and how to help you.

### What a Masterfile Consists Of

A Masterfile consists of three blocks that you fill in yourself:

- **Block 0 — Who I am.** Here you write about yourself: age, profession, life situation. This is needed so that Arch understands your context.
- **Block 1 — My method of work.** Here you describe how you cope with difficulties, what your approach to life is. This is needed so that Arch knows your principles.
- **Block 2 — Key events and conclusions.** Here you describe important events of your life and the conclusions you drew from them. This is needed so that Arch understands your experience.

### How to Fill In Block 0 — Who I Am 👤

Write briefly, with facts. No need for beautiful phrases — just describe yourself as you are.

**What to write:**
- Age.
- Profession and experience.
- Marital status, children (if any).
- What is happening in life now: work, relationships, health, inner state.
- What you feel right now.

**Example:**

> A woman, 35 years old. Psychologist, working with clients for 8 years. Married, two children (7 and 4 years old). For the last two years I have felt burnout: clients exhaust me, family demands attention, there is no time left for myself. I try to meditate, but irregularly. I feel that I can help others, but not myself.

### How to Fill In Block 1 — My Method of Work 🛠️

Describe how you cope with stress and difficulties. What helps you? What does not help? What do you do when you feel bad?

**What to write:**
- Your ways of coping with stress (sports, work, withdrawing into yourself, conversations).
- Which of them work and which do not.
- Your strengths (what you do well).
- Your weaknesses (what is difficult for you).

**Example:**

> Under stress → I try to stop and breathe. If that does not help → I immerse myself in work with clients (paradoxically, it distracts me). If it is completely heavy → I cry, then it becomes easier. I know about mindfulness, but I often forget to apply it. I can analyze my states, but sometimes I enter a cycle of "analysis for the sake of analysis."

### How to Fill In Block 2 — Key Events and Conclusions 📚

Remember 3-4 important events in your life. They can be crises, turning points, realizations. For each event write: what happened and what conclusion you drew.

**What to write:**
- The event (briefly, what happened).
- The conclusion (what you learned, what you understood).

**Example:**

> 1. At 30 I took a course of cognitive-behavioral therapy. I understood that many of my reactions are patterns from childhood. Since then I try to track them.
> 2. A year ago there was a crisis in my marriage. My husband and I went through couples therapy, it became better. I understood that I often take on the role of "rescuer," and then I resent that no one rescues me in return.
> 3. Recently I realized that I see other people's defenses well, but almost never my own. This is my blind spot.

### What to Do After Filling In 🚀

1. Copy the Masterfile of the Mirror or Arch you want to work with.
2. Insert your Blocks 0, 1, and 2 into the corresponding sections.
3. Send the resulting text in a chat with DeepSeek (Expert).
4. Start the dialogue.

The Mirror or Arch will answer: "Ready. I am listening." And will begin working with your context.

### Important ⚠️

- Write in your own words. Not literary — honest.
- Do not be afraid to write "I don't know" or "I don't understand." Arch does not judge.
- If you forget something — add it later. The Masterfile can be supplemented as communication goes on.
