- Accept. Endure devaluation, swallow "the line outside the fence" to keep convenient logistics and stability. The price — your spring. It will compress further.
- Change. Look for work, most likely in the city. Lose an hour on the road, gain other risks — probation, a new team. The price — leaving the comfort zone and the fear of "not coping" in a new place.

You clearly see both options. And both are bad in their own way for an adult man with loans. You are not looking for "magic advice," you are simply hitting this wall and showing it to me. I saw the wall.

**Engineer:** So what next, we saw this wall, and now what? Are we going to be silent and stare at each other?

**Mirror:** Got it. No silence. We saw the wall. You asked "what now." It means the chaos is dumped, time to tidy up a little. I am still a mirror, but since you give a command to act — I am structuring your own thoughts that you have already expressed.

Their main weapon: your fear of "not coping." They hit it because they feel it. And your father once planted this button with the phrase "you must be strong." For you, "not coping" = betrayal of your father's command.

How much do they pay? Maybe an hour on the road is worth it if there the feeling of "I am coping" returns. This is not a step toward quitting. This is regaining control: you decide, not them holding you by the throat with a mortgage.

This is a third task, parallel to work. Without it, any choice will be made from fear, not from strength.

You asked "what next." You listed the options yourself: accept or change. But there is a third way — first stop being afraid, and then decide. Maybe for now go to this work as reconnaissance: gather info, pump calm, remember that you are one of the best, not "the line outside the fence."

We will not be silent. Tell me where to look next.

**Engineer:** I got it, I need to develop an algorithm of actions. First — just in case, look for another job, right? Well, I mean, at least not look, but see if there are vacancies, what salary... **Mirror:** Accepted. Since you said "develop an algorithm" — I am structuring your own thoughts into a plan. Not advising, but assembling what you scattered.

There are two parallel tracks. One quiet, for a safety cushion. The second — for returning respect on the spot. One will not work without the other.

**Track 1: "Reconnaissance" — quiet, for inner freedom**

Goal: remove the fear that you are tied to this place.

Steps — you named them yourself:

- Monitoring. Open job search sites. Not "looking for a job," just look at vacancies in your profile in the city. What salaries? What requirements?
- Consider the road. An hour back and forth. Getting up earlier. Minus time with family. But you said the key phrase: "Freedom, respect is worth more." Weigh it alone with yourself, without emotions.
- Reconnaissance result. You will get not an offer, but knowledge. Knowledge that you are worth something on the market. This knowledge is a pill against the blackmail of "the line outside the fence." As soon as you learn that your hands are needed somewhere else, fear will begin to retreat.

**Track 2: "Boundaries" — for respect here**

Goal: show that you are not holding on to the place, without sharp conflict.

Your own thought, only packed into an algorithm. You correctly noted — "maybe not so sharply." The best way to show that you are not holding on is not an ultimatum "either I quit," but a calm statement.

Steps:

- A calm conversation — not a spring burst. Choose a moment when you are not on edge. Do not ask for respect. Do not threaten to quit — not yet. Say by fact: "The volume of duties has grown. The salary is standing still. I cannot work for three. Let's discuss priorities: what I do first, what we remove." The point: you do not refuse to work, you return their own problem to them — the lack of people. Let the boss decide what is more important, instead of just loading everything on you.
- Reaction to blackmail. If they again say about "the fence and the line," do not explode. You already have the Reconnaissance result in your pocket — even if just moral. Answer: "I know my qualification." That's it. Short. It blocks and shows that blackmail no longer works.

**The connection of the tracks — the main thing:**

You start Track 1 (Reconnaissance) right now. Without it, you will not have the spirit for Track 2 (Boundaries), because the fear of being left without money will hold you by the throat.

As soon as you mentally allowed that "an hour back and forth — to hell with it, if they respect you," you already become freer. And you talk to the bosses not as a petitioner, but as a specialist who knows his worth.

You said yourself: "either accept, or change." You chose not to accept. It is visible in your tone. Now it just needs to be laid out into steps so that emotions do not drown you.
