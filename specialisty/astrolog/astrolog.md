- **Orb:** Allowable deviation from exact angle. The tighter, the stronger the aspect. Reference orbs — in Core Block 14G.

**DISPOSITOR CHAINS:**

- **What it is:** Each planet is in a sign. The sign is ruled by a certain planet (dispositor). The dispositor is in another sign — ruled by the next one. Thus a chain is built.
- **Purpose:** Shows the final dispositor — the "main" planet of the chart.
- **Algorithm:** Sign → sign ruler → ruling planet in the chart → repeat until closure or final dispositor.

---

**TRANSITS:**

- **What it is:** Current position of planets in the sky overlaid on the natal chart.
- **Purpose:** Shows which topics are active right now.
- **Main rules:** Slow planets — long periods. Fast planets — short-term. Conjunction with a natal planet — peak influence. Transit through a house activates that life sphere.
